When the content spans multiple posts:
- Divide by paragraphs as much as possible — not every sentence, but enough to stay easily readable.
- Try to fit a single idea in one post.
- It's fine to keep a post short if the next part is a different idea — move it to the next post rather than cram two ideas together. A one-sentence post is fine when that sentence is its own idea.
- If a passage exceeds the character limit and genuinely cannot be split into posts in a neat way, break it after a comma, end the post with ↓, and continue in the next post.
